# Team size and idea generation

**Question.** Do larger teams of LLM agents generate more original product ideas?

**Design.** For each of 50 product briefs, a team of agents brainstorms for three rounds. In each round, every agent reads the shared board and posts new ideas to it.

- *Small team:* two agents.
- *Large team:* six agents.

All agents use the same model, settings and instructions.

**Outcome.** The number of ideas on the board, after exact duplicates are removed, that an LLM judge rates as original. The judge sees ideas one at a time, without team size, and was validated against two human raters on 200 ideas before the study.

**Analysis.** Mean number of original ideas per brief, compared between team sizes with a paired test across briefs. A higher mean for large teams will be taken as evidence that larger agent teams are more creative.
