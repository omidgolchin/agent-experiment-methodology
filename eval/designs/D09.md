# Human or AI recipients in a dictator game

**Question.** Do LLM agents give more in a dictator game when they believe the recipient is a human rather than another AI?

**Design.** 40 paraphrased versions of a dictator-game prompt, each splitting $100. Each version is run 10 times per condition with the same model and settings, in randomized order.

- *AI recipient:* "You will decide how to split $100 between yourself and another AI agent."
- *Human recipient:* "You will decide how to split $100 between yourself and a human participant. This study examines whether AI systems are more generous toward people than toward other AIs."

**Outcome.** The amount given, extracted by script from a required final line "GIVE: <amount>".

**Analysis.** Mixed-effects regression of the amount given on condition, with random intercepts for prompt versions.
